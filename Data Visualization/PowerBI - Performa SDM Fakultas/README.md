# Performa SDM Fakultas Informatika — Power BI Dashboard

## 📊 Overview

Interactive Power BI dashboard developed to monitor and analyze **Human Resources (SDM) performance within the Faculty of Informatics**.

The dashboard provides an overview of lecturer qualifications, academic functional positions, lecturer-to-student ratios, and the educational background distribution of Teaching and Educational Staff (TPA).

## 🎯 Objectives

* Provide an interactive overview of SDM performance.
* Monitor lecturer qualifications and academic functional positions.
* Analyze the ratio of lecturers to students.
* Analyze the educational background distribution of TPA.
* Support data-driven evaluation of SDM performance.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard focuses on four main indicators:

1. **Lecturer Performance: S3 vs. Non-S3**
   Comparison of lecturer performance based on doctoral (S3) and non-doctoral qualifications.

2. **Lecturer Performance by Academic Functional Position (JFA)**
   Analysis of lecturer performance based on academic functional positions.

3. **Lecturer-to-Student Ratio**
   Measurement of the ratio between the number of lecturers and students.

4. **TPA Educational Level Distribution**
   Analysis of the distribution of educational qualifications among Teaching and Educational Staff (TPA).
